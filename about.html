About ForgePoint

Practical engineering for industrial operations

ForgePoint Engineering is an engineering consultancy concept focused on mechanical systems, asset reliability and industrial projects for organisations across the United Kingdom.

Our philosophy

Sound engineering, applied with care

We believe good engineering starts with understanding how equipment is really used — not how it looks on paper.

Industrial plant rarely operates in ideal conditions. Loads change, duties drift from the original design and maintenance windows are tight. Our approach is to look closely at the evidence, ask practical questions and recommend solutions that operations and maintenance teams can actually put to work.

We aim to give clear, honest technical advice: explaining the reasoning behind each recommendation, being open about uncertainty and focusing on improvements that are proportionate to the problem.

Engineering performance. Built to last.

Our guiding principle: solutions that keep working long after the project has closed.

Our engineering approach

A structured process from first look to long-term performance

Every engagement follows the same four stages, scaled to the size and complexity of the challenge.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Visit site, review documentation and talk to the people who run and maintain the equipment to understand the problem in context.

  2. 02

    Analyse

    Examine operating data, failure history and design information to identify root causes and the options available.

  3. 03

    Engineer

    Develop and document a practical solution, from design changes and specifications to revised maintenance plans.

  4. 04

    Sustain

    Support implementation, hand over clearly and help put measures in place so improvements last.

Areas of expertise

Where we focus

Our work centres on the mechanical equipment and systems that industrial operations depend on day to day.
